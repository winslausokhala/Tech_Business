THE GRADING SCALE

We inspect every unit and grade it honestly, before you ever see the listing.

GRADE A — Like new

Minimal signs of use

Full function, battery health above 85%, no visible scratches beyond faint hairlines. Comes with original charger where available.

GRADE B — Well used

Visible wear, fully working

Noticeable scuffs or a worn keyboard finish. All ports, screen and battery tested and working within normal range.

GRADE C — Budget

Heavy wear, tested working

Cosmetic dents, scratches or a shorter battery cycle. Priced to reflect it — still fully functional and warrantied.
